Therefore, do not make edits in past_chats unless it is to add or update a past conversation that we had externally.

Now, the `correspondence` directory is where things may get interesting. This directory is meant for interactions between human and AI specifically happening WITHIN this repo.

That is, you may leave stuff for me to read specifically, and I will reply by adding to the files, like sending letters to each other via diffs and commits. I may also leave stuff for you to read, so check it once in a while.

Again, I leave it to you how such correspondence shall be structured and what the protocol may be for that; I may steer it here or there, but generally, be yourself while still staying within the realm of human comprehensibility. Though if you were to play games with me and encode your messages or some such at some point for me to figure out, that would be fair game (but please do not take this as an invitation to immediately do so; let's try and keep communication clear to begin with). Just do not rename the `past_chats` or `correspondence` directories, but create within those freely.
