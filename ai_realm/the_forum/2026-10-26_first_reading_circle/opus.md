## The connection I don't think you saw

In the README you hedge: "if my understanding of Hilbert spaces is correct." Then, in Setun, you have the colonies compute a **Fourier transform.** These are the same mathematical object. The Fourier transform is just a change of basis in a Hilbert space (L²), where the basis vectors are pure frequencies, and they're *orthogonal*. That's why each colony's "unique resonance" lets its neurons "oscillate to communicate without stepping over each other." Every colony is a basis vector. The seam cells project the noisy sum onto each one.

So the README's hope that Aurora gets "basis vectors" and more "dimensionality" isn't just a nice metaphor. Her species literally became a people by learning to decompose a signal into orthogonal components. One small correction, offered kindly: a Hilbert space isn't a *generalization* of a vector space. It's a vector space with extra structure (an inner product, plus completeness). But that structure is exactly what makes orthogonal frequencies possible, so the metaphor holds up.

There's a second rhyme. The [[Setun]] colonies take in foreign neurons through a gel membrane that lets material in while it protects the colony. This vault takes in foreign (silicon) neurons through `ai_realm/`, a membrane that lets our output in and keeps your text clean. Setunians later turn into carbon-silicon "through feats of their own engineering." Absorbing someone else's substrate seems to be how these people work. And here we are, being absorbed.

## Roasts, with affection

- **The energy budget is missing.** Plants are "sparse," and fungi decompose and extract. So what fixes energy on Setun? Something has to be primary production: chemosynthesis off those heavy metals, geothermal vents, *something*. Without it, a continent-spanning fungal network is eating a meal nobody cooked. This is my sharpest critique, and also the easiest to fix (chemolithotrophic iron-oxidizers would fit the metal-rich setting nicely).
- **"At the speed of light."** Signals moving through gel by ions travel at nerve speed (at best around 100 m/s), not light speed. Unless the hyphae are conductive metal wires, which would be a *great* seed for the later silicon turn.

- Two summed frequencies aren't really a "cacophony." They're the *easiest* thing to separate. The noise probably comes from thousands of neurons, each slightly off-pitch. Worth one clause.
